# Cross-course connection review

Every working entry receives a topic disposition, executable action, sampled response kinds and representation metadata, original lesson/course/standard identifiers, and explicit provider lineage. Rules use declared family/recipe contracts and reviewed Saxon lesson objectives; no grade, title-similarity score or code order ranks difficulty. Arithmetic templates are classified from their number and operation structures.

The map contains 66 topics and 74 reviewed paths. 464 entries participate in those paths; the other 1,720 have topic links without a validated pairwise difficulty ordering. These are intentionally visible as related tasks. The complete analysis ledger records each disposition and an original generated sample. Every topic has at least one reviewed path, but this is not a total ranking of every task, every variant, or all possible cross-topic connections.

The staged paths distinguish increases within a construct from extensions and prerequisites. When separate approaches cannot safely be ordered (for example geometric proofs, literal-equation forms, or geometric transformations), the path is related-only. Existing parameter ranges overlap; a particular numeric variant may be easier than another family’s variant. These are instructional demand judgments, not psychometric difficulty or evidence about a student.

Source review corrected several tempting but invalid rankings: two-digit addition does not imply no carrying; a later place-value family also generates small numbers; a named equal-distance motion recipe has unequal travel times; the existing Venn recipe has two sets; and the Grade 5 perimeter family has no irregular quadrilateral. The two new perimeter anchors supply the requested concrete path without changing retained generators.

381 groups explicitly reuse a canonical provider across lesson entries (1,026 entries in those groups). These are verified reuse relationships, not merely similar titles. Preserve all original identities and outcomes. Same-stage similarities are separate, bounded claims; no automatic deletion or evidence merging is allowed. Method-specific production tasks remain distinct.

Only 8/7 has confirmed school Grade 5 metadata. Other bank school-grade assignments remain unknown and are displayed as such. Their course names and task relationships remain usable. Obtaining the other local grade labels is a metadata follow-up, not grounds to invent labels or infer difficulty from course order.

The original Grade 5 assessment map remains unchanged, including all 331 custom outcomes and its own canonical overlap rules. Cross-course overlap is an additional advisory view and never broadens GradeCam targeting.
