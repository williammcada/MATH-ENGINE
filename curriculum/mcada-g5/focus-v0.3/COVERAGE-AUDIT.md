Phase 1 verifies task implementation and availability. It does not certify that one bounded task exhausts every facet of an outcome. All 331 full-outcome and automatic-assignment acceptance flags remain false. Phase 2 reviews breadth, variation, cross-lesson duplication and curriculum acceptance. Phase 3 delivers tests/practice, targeted Grade 5 packets, printing and exports.
