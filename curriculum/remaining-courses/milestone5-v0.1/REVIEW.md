## Source decisions

Private source hash is recorded in the change specification. Recovered SD 922/923 uses the population denominator N=5; new prompts state this, including all-equal data. No sample-SD claim. Mixture 492 removes pure solute; this differs from mixed-solution removal/replacement, supplied separately. Fixed-stock tasks ask for total final volume; fixed-final tasks ask for stronger stock amount. Gas questions use positive Kelvin temperatures, 273.15 when Celsius is supplied, explicit constants/units and final-only rounding. Decimal gas tasks in M5 do not claim completion of legacy significant-figure/scientific-notation method demands; those are explicit M6 variants.

Proofs require complete diagrams and reasoning; constructions require actual marks. The finite proof/construction recipes are theorem/method archetypes: changing labels alone would not create a new mathematical demand. Some are intentionally stable tasks. Additional source-specific proof and construction variants remain in M6. Plane inequality references are answer-only; students must draw/shade. Family anchors retain fullOutcomeVerified=false.
