# Comparison Chunk 2 quickstart

Open `downloads/Test-and-Practice-Studio-v0.32.0-rc.2.html` in a browser. The download is self-contained and works locally.

1. Choose a course and preview a working task, then select **Find related tasks**.
2. Filter by course and relation. **Easier/harder** means a reviewed change within a skill; **prerequisite/extension** adds a construct. **Related** does not imply a difficulty rank.
3. Preview a suggestion, add it to the draft, or use it as the starting task to move in either direction. Changing comparison filters does not change the draft. Remove and Undo remain available.
4. Generate the mixed-bank packet and export student pages or a teacher key. Use one column for large graph-choice questions; the layout guard rejects a question that cannot fit. Two columns work for suitably compact tasks.

New examples include one-operation equations leading to two-step equations, line equations from a supplied slope versus two points, rational-domain checks, root methods, logarithmic equations, function-domain combinations and contextual systems. Shared-provider warnings identify reuse without removing a teacher's selection.
