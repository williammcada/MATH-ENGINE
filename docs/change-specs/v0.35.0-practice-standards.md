# Math Engine 0.35.0 — standards in the practice catalog and a positive-result guard for 8/7 9.6

**Status:** Approved by William McAda, 2026-10-15 (Asia/Shanghai): "Proceed to fix." Implementation checkpoint; not a verified release until the checks below pass on the exact committed candidate.
**Date:** 2026-10-15
**Baseline:** MATH-ENGINE `main` `5414be6` (Engine 0.34.0-rc.3).
**Consumer:** Math Arcade v0.9.0 (skill search redesign) pins the resulting bundle.

## Problems found

1. `src/practice.js` built every course-bank catalog entry with `standard: ''`. Searching a host such as Math Arcade for `PS.MAT.G5.NF.9.6` returned nothing, although the bank catalog records that standard.
2. The 8/7 Lesson 9.6 generator in `src/curriculum87-phase2.js` (order of operations with fractions) used subtraction branches without a sign check. In a 400-seed sample, 66 answers were negative and 1 was zero (for example `(1/8 − 1/5) × 5/6 = −1/16`). Negative numbers are out of scope for this Grade 5 skill.

## Changes

- **Standards:** each practice catalog bank entry now carries `standard` and a new `standardAlias` copied from the course-bank catalog (`src/course-banks.js`). Entries without a recorded outcome keep `''`; no standard is invented. Coverage at this baseline: 228 of 1,441 game-eligible bank entries, all in Introduction to PreAlgebra (8/7). Intermediate 4, Course 1, Algebra 1/2, Algebra 1 and Algebra 2 have no standards recorded in the Engine.
- **9.6 guard:** subtraction branches redraw their three fractions (up to 64 seeded attempts) until the result is positive, with a guaranteed fallback first fraction of 7/8. Items whose answer was already positive are unchanged for the same seed and index.
- Version 0.35.0-rc.1; `dist/math-engine-practice.js` and provenance rebuilt.

## Not changed

Question text, answer checking, IDs, lesson labels, the 312 K–7 skills, and teacher-reviewed tasks. The Grade 5 provider families in `src/grade5.js` (including `g5.fraction-expression-precedence`) remain outside the practice bundle; adding them is a separate decision.

- `npm test` and `npm run test:phase2`.
- Known pre-existing failure: `tests/curriculum87.cjs` asserts 891 bank entries and fails the same way on unchanged `5414be6` (bank has 2,184). Not caused by this change; recorded, not fixed here.
